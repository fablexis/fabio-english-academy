# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Spanish-speaking adults (Latin America, primarily Argentina based on the WhatsApp number) who want to improve their English for real-life situations: work, interviews, travel, daily conversation. They browse on mobile and desktop, often arriving from social media (TikTok, YouTube). They are evaluating whether this tutoring service feels approachable, professional, and worth messaging on WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

"Your English Buddy" is a personal English tutoring service. The site's job is to convert visitors into WhatsApp conversations: book a class, ask about courses, take the diagnostic. Secondary job: build trust and authority through the blog (12 practical English articles) and the about page. Success = WhatsApp clicks and returning blog readers.

## Brand Personality

Friendly, energetic, confidence-building. A "buddy", not an academy: warm and human, never institutional or intimidating. The tone says "you can do this, and it can be fun." Visually bold and playful (bright lime + deep teal from the printed brochure), but the teaching content is serious and practical.

## Anti-references

- Generic academy/institute sites: stocky, corporate, navy-blazer formality.
- Duolingo clones: don't mimic the owl-app gamified look; this is a human tutor.
- SaaS landing-page grammar: hero metrics, identical feature card grids, cream backgrounds.
- Cluttered "infoproduct" sales pages with countdown timers and aggressive urgency.

## Design Principles

1. **The buddy is human**: photography, the character, and first-person warmth beat abstract illustration. Every section should feel like a person teaching, not a platform selling.
2. **Confidence through play**: motion and color do emotional work; they make English feel approachable. Playful, but every animation has a purpose.
3. **One destination: WhatsApp**: every page should make the next step (message the tutor) obvious and low-friction.
4. **Teach on the page**: blog content and micro-copy can demonstrate the teaching style (clear, bilingual-aware, practical examples).
5. **Brochure-true color**: teal #185C60, lime #C8E47C, green CTA #5CF890 are the committed identity; deepen and extend them, never replace them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast targets for all body text.
- Full `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for every animation.
- Keyboard navigation and focus states on all interactive elements (modals already support ESC + scroll lock; keep that bar).
- Spanish UI copy throughout; English appears only as taught content.
